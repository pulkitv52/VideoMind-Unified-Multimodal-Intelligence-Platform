Suggested UI Enhancements for VideoMind

Key design proposals to elevate the current user interface from a prototype layout to a production-ready Multimodal AI intelligence dashboard.

1. Visual Hierarchy & Theme
Modern Slate Dark & Light Palettes

Replace the austere flat greenish background with an executive slate/obsidian dark mode, paired with vibrant emerald and electric cyan accents for high readability during prolonged research sessions.

2. Unified Omnibar Search
Cross-Modal Search & Filter Pills

Consolidate scattered inputs into a clean central Omnibar with inline keyboard shortcuts, instant modality chips, and visual dropzones for image-based reverse search.

3. Synchronized Evidence Inspector
Side-by-Side Verification Workbench

Elevate the evidence inspection pane with audio/video waveform scrubbers, interactive karaoke-style transcript word highlighting, and visual bounding boxes over scanned PDF pages.

4. Glassmorphic Elevation & Feedback
Micro-Interactions & Status Feedback

Add subtle backdrop blurs, crisp borders, pulsating status indicators for Celery/PostgreSQL vector sync, and one-click citation copying with animated toasts.
